With the 2-deck **Hercules DJControl Compact** measuring at just 13.4 x 3.9 inches makes it one of the most **compact and portable** [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) available. Our DJ controller ‘mapping and testing’ team feels **the DJControl Compact’s slim design makes it a perfect option for practicing or for light DJing** at small gatherings and house parties. Or, in the event your main DJ controller fails at a gig it could fill in as a useful backup.

While relatively minuscule compared to many of the other DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE [supported DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) this little guy packs a punch. The **Hercules DJControl Compact** pads (4 for each deck) control loops, manipulates effects, trigger samples and cue points. Each deck is also equipped with a two-band equalizer and 3″ jog wheels that work well enough for some basic scratching.

One item missing that’s become somewhat standard in most DJ controller is the **lack of a built-in audio interface**. If you need headphone pre-fade listening you could use the **Hercules DJControl Compact** with an audio interface such as the [Focusrite Scarlett 2i4](https://us.focusrite.com/usb-audio-interfaces/scarlett-2i4).

If you’re a DJ on the go that values portability without too much sacrifice the **Hercules DJControl Compact** pairs well with DEX 3 and DEX 3 [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). I can see some old PCDJ DAC-3 controller users enjoy DJing with it.
