**Download the user submitted ‘White Out’ skin for DEX 3, DEX 3 RE and DEX 3 LE DJ mixing software.**

We’re entrenched in the dog days of summer. It’s hotter than an American setting a thermostat in Europe and often very bright outside.

Those of us that take on jobs where we have to DJ outside in the sunshine have to deal with screen glare – to the point even young eyes have issues making out buttons, sliders and knobs on the default skins (GUI) included with DEX 3, DEX 3 RE and DEX LE ([Free DJ Software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)).

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![DEX 3 DayTime Skin](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/dex3re-daytimeskin.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "DEX 3 'White Out' Skin")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/dex3re-daytimeskin.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

A [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJ was dealing with those exact conditions during her weekly beach party gig on the Jersey Shore. She took it upon herself to edit the default [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) skin, using a lighter color pallet that almost entirely negates screen glare issues.

The ‘White Out’ skin harkens back to the original RED Mobile (1.1) daytime skin and we’re sure it will prove useful on those outdoor gigs where your DJ computer is bathing in sunlight.

Anyone can edit the existing skin designs of any of our **DJ mixing software** products. You can make simple adjustments by opening up the skin files (located in the program folder) in any graphics editing application using hue and color to customize the skin. Or, you can build a new **DJ software skin** design from scratch and map it using our [Free Skin Designer](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) software.

Users can **submit skins to the PCDJ community** by emailing their finished design to **Submit (@) PCDJ (Dot) Com**.

[DOWNLOAD WHITE OUT SKIN FOR ALL VERSIONS OF DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

**NOTE:** Unzip the contents of the zip file to Documents -> PCDJ-DEX3 (or PCDJ-DEX3LE, PCDJ-RED3) -> Skins. Then you can select and enable the skin in the DJ mixing software options under the “select user interface” drop-down menu under the general tab.
